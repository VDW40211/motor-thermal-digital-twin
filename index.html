Engineering Transparency

Engineering Assumptions
  • Loss segregation Assumed: copper 60%, core 20%, mechanical 10%, stray 10% of total rated losses (editable above).
  • Hot-spot allowance Assumed: 10 K above average winding temperature (IEC 60034-1 methodology).
  • Core loss scaling Derived: ~ (V/Vrated)² · (f/frated)1.5.
  • Friction & windage scaling Derived: ~ (n/nrated)2.5.
  • TEFC cooling effectiveness Derived: ~ (n/nrated)1.5, with a low-speed floor.
  • Voltage imbalance derating Derived: piecewise-linear per NEMA MG-1.
  • Insulation life Typical: "10 K rise ≈ half life" rule of thumb — not a validated life prediction.
Model Limitations
  • Single-node (first-order) lumped thermal model; real machines exhibit multiple thermal time constants.
  • Winding and frame temperatures are not separated in v1.
  • VFD harmonic losses and inrush/startup heating are not modeled.
  • Duty cycles are approximated as continuous (S1).
  • Altitude above 1000 m is not corrected.
  • Frequent-start detection is rule-based only; thermal memory of start events is not accumulated.
